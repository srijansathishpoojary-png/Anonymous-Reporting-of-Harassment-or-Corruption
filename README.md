# Anonymous Reporting

A secure and confidential platform for reporting **harassment, misconduct, corruption, fraud, or other unethical behavior** without requiring you to reveal your identity.

## Purpose

This platform provides a safe channel for individuals to report concerns that may otherwise go unreported because of fear of retaliation, embarrassment, or lack of access to trusted reporting channels.

Reports can help organizations identify and address:

* Harassment or bullying
* Discrimination or abuse
* Corruption or bribery
* Fraud or financial misconduct
* Conflicts of interest
* Abuse of authority
* Workplace or organizational misconduct
* Other violations of policies, laws, or ethical standards

## Anonymous Reporting

You may submit a report without providing your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information.

To help protect your anonymity:

* Avoid including your name or personal details unless necessary.
* Do not include identifying information in uploaded files.
* Describe events objectively and provide relevant facts.
* Consider using a private device or network if you are concerned about being identified through network or device records.

> **Important:** No online system can guarantee absolute anonymity in every circumstance. Review the platform's privacy and security practices before submitting sensitive information.

## What to Include in a Report

A useful report should include as much relevant information as you can safely provide:

**What happened?**
Describe the incident or suspected misconduct.

**When did it happen?**
Provide dates and approximate times when possible.

**Where did it happen?**
Include the relevant location, department, project, or platform.

**Who was involved?**
Identify individuals, teams, or organizations involved when relevant.

**What evidence exists?**
Provide documents, messages, screenshots, transaction records, or other evidence when it is safe and lawful to do so.

**Are there witnesses?**
Include names or other identifying information only when appropriate and necessary.

## After Submitting a Report

Your report should receive a unique **report ID or reference number**.

Keep this reference number somewhere safe. It may allow you to:

* Check the status of your report
* Receive follow-up questions
* Provide additional information
* Respond to investigators while remaining anonymous

If the system provides an anonymous mailbox or secure messaging feature, use it for follow-up communication rather than revealing your identity.

## False or Malicious Reports

Reports should be submitted in good faith.

Knowingly submitting false accusations, fabricating evidence, or using the reporting system to harass another person may undermine legitimate investigations and may violate applicable policies or laws.

You do **not** need to prove misconduct before submitting a good-faith report. Provide the facts you know and clearly distinguish them from assumptions or speculation.

## Privacy & Security

The platform should minimize the collection of personal information and protect submitted reports against unauthorized access.

Administrators should establish appropriate controls for:

* Encryption in transit and at rest
* Access control and authentication
* Secure evidence storage
* Audit logging
* Data retention and deletion
* Protection against unauthorized disclosure
* Secure handling of reporter communications

## Emergency Situations

This platform is **not intended for emergencies** or situations involving an immediate threat to someone's life or safety.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services or authorities.

## Responsible Use

By using this platform, please:

1. Submit reports honestly and in good faith.
2. Provide accurate information to the best of your knowledge.
3. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
4. Do not submit information that you are not legally permitted to disclose.
5. Preserve your report reference number for future communication.

## Mission

The goal of anonymous reporting is to make it easier for people to speak up about misconduct while reducing fear of retaliation and protecting the integrity of the reporting process.

**Speak up. Stay safe. Help create a more accountable environment.**
